Joy Bhattacharyya, Ph.D.

Hello! I am an astronomer interested in galaxy evolution, particularly in the context of dwarf galaxies and the circumgalactic medium. My aim is to understand the dark matter and baryonic physics that shape this evolution. I am adept in both observational and simulations-based techniques. My journey of research in astronomy has taken me across domains stretching from blazars, dark matter, the circumgalactic medium and satellite galaxies.

About Me

I use my passion for math and physics to understand how the Universe works. I was drawn to galaxy evolution after a tryst in X-ray astronomy, I discovered the joy of studying galaxy evolution that was driven mainly by my love for dwarf galaxies.

Analysis

I use large-scale datasets from survey observations including DECaLS and cosmological simulations like IllustrisTNG to study the evolution of dwarf galaxies across different masses and epochs.
